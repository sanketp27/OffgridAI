## Retail & Commerce theme wording
From the official themes page, the Retail & Commerce theme is described as:

> "Retail & Commerce: Intelligent Customer and Business Experiences"
>
> "Build an AI-powered solution that transforms how businesses understand, engage, and serve customers while improving operational efficiency across physical and digital commerce. Solutions may address discovery, personalization, conversational shopping, demand planning, inventory, customer insights, or fraud."
>
> Source: https://aibuildercup.com/themes.html

## Required submission components, format, and deadlines
The public site states that submissions must be a working prototype, not just a pitch deck or mockup. It also says submissions must use Google AI models or agentic platforms and be deployed on Google Cloud via Cloud Run or Firebase.

Official submission/format guidance from the FAQ page:
- A working deployed link of the prototype
- A video demo under 3 minutes
- A public GitHub repository for the prototype
- A deck explaining the solution in detail
- One team submits one solution under one theme/problem statement
- The project must be built during the hackathon timeline; pre-existing projects are not eligible

Official timeline from the home page:
- Registration & Team Formation: 1 Sept - 11 Oct, 2026
- Prototype Building & Submission: 7 Sept - 18 Oct, 2026
- Prototype submission deadline: Oct 18, 2026
- Evaluation Phase: Oct 19 - Nov 6, 2026
- Finalists Announcement: Nov 7, 2026
- Grand Finale (Demo Day): Dec 4, 2026, Singapore

## Judging criteria and weights
The same four weighted criteria apply across all six themes, per the official FAQ and themes page:
- Technical Merit & Gen AI Implementation — 40%
  - Meaningful use of Gen AI
  - Technical implementation, scalability, and sustainability
- Problem Alignment & Impact — 25%
  - Problem alignment and potential impact
- Innovation & Creativity — 25%
  - Originality, differentiation, and creative Gen AI use
- User Experience & Solution Design — 10%
  - Usability, accessibility, and cohesive experience

Source: https://aibuildercup.com/themes.html and https://aibuildercup.com/Faqs.html

## Ambiguities / limits of the public guidance
- The public site gives the main judging rubric and submission requirements, but it does not publish a standalone formal rules PDF or a step-by-step submission checklist beyond the FAQ and theme pages.
- The FAQ says the submission format includes a deck and repo, but it does not specify exact file naming conventions or a formal upload workflow on the public pages themselves.
- The public guidance is official first-party content from the event site, but any final submission portal instructions may be handled elsewhere during the active registration/build window.
